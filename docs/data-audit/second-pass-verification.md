**Arbiter ruling — SKEPTIC CORRECT** on: _App's J&K total of 46% (no RBA/ALC) is correct_

For direct recruitment to J&K UT government posts as of 22 Oct 2026, vertical reservation totals 60% and open merit is 40%. The J&K Reservation Act 2004 and Reservation Rules 2005, as amended by S.O. 176 of 15.03.2024, set the split.
- SC 8
- ST 20 (ST-1 10 and ST-2 10)
- OBC 8
- RBA (Residents of Backward Areas) 10
- ALC/IB (residents along the Actual Line of Control / International Border) 4
- EWS 10, outside the 50% cap

The app's figures of 46% reserved and 54% "OTHERS" are wrong. Its `OTHERS` value should be 40.

**How to model RBA and ALC/IB:** store them as two separate vertical categories, not inside OBC. Nesting them under OBC would make J&K's "OBC" read 22% and break comparisons with other states. The 2004 Act may legally group RBA, ALC and the former "social castes" (now OBC) as socially and educationally backward classes. That comes from my own knowledge and I did not confirm it. If it is true, the skeptic's nesting option can be described in the notes but should not be used for the OBC figure.

**Horizontal quotas:** ex-servicemen 6% and persons with disabilities 4% are horizontal and should not be modelled as vertical. The "30% open merit" figure in the press subtracts them.

**Pending change:** a cabinet proposal approved on 03.12.2025 would cut EWS from 10 to 3 and RBA from 10 to 7, making the vertical total 50%. As of my searches it has not been notified: the government told the Assembly in Feb 2026 that it was "under examination by the Competent Authority", and in Jun 2026 the J&K Cabinet cleared replies to MHA's queries. The notes should mention it, but the figures should not change.
